# 🌟 An Unforgettable Experience at the IDE Bootcamp!

**Dates Attended:** 17th – 21st February, 2025  


![IDE Bootcamp Banner](https://source.unsplash.com/1200x400/?startup,innovation,teamwork)

From **17th to 21st February**, I had the incredible opportunity to attend the **Innovation Design Entrepreneurship (IDE) Bootcamp** at *Amity University, Jharkhand, Ranchi*—an initiative by **AICTE, MOE Innovation Cell, and the Government of India**.  

It was a world-class learning experience, where we dived deep into the **startup ecosystem**, **incubation process**, and **business innovation**.

---

## 🚀 Incubation Tour at STPI
One of the most exciting parts was our guided incubation tour to **STPI**, where we:

- Witnessed startups being built from scratch  
- Explored a **server room** 🔧  
- Gained fascinating insights into the infrastructure supporting cutting-edge innovations  

![Incubation Tour](https://source.unsplash.com/800x400/?server,technology,innovation)

---

## 👥 Team MindPal’s VR Innovation
As part of **Team MindPal** *(Ahir Sarkar, Sylvia Barick, Soumee Bhaumik, Sarvani Saha, and myself)*, we worked on a **VR hardware project for stress relief**, targeting specific nerves in the brain.  

On the final day, we **pitched our idea** and showcased our **low-fidelity prototype** to industry experts, receiving **valuable feedback** to refine and improve our concept.

![VR Innovation](https://source.unsplash.com/800x400/?vr,headset,innovation)

---

## 🙌 Heartfelt Gratitude
A huge thank you to our mentor **Dibakar Roy Choudhury** for guiding us at every step, and special thanks to **Debangshu Chatterjee** for the constant support and motivation.  

This journey was not just about learning but also about **collaboration, innovation, and networking** with teams from different institutions.
