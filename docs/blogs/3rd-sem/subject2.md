# ICYIM 2025 – Industry Connect Young Innovators Meet  
### Runners-Up | Startup Pitch Recognition by The IET

## Event Overview
- **Event:** Industry Connect Young Innovators Meet (ICYIM 2025)  
- **Organized by:** The Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET)  
- **Achievement:** Runners-Up  
- **Category:** Startup Pitch / Innovation  

While many around me were celebrating college-level recognitions, I chose a quieter path — one focused on learning, failing, rebuilding, and challenging myself on a larger stage.

After multiple setbacks and iterations, I am proud to share that our team secured the **Runners-Up position at ICYIM 2025**, organized by **The IET**. This recognition marked a significant milestone in our journey as innovators and builders.

---

## Startup Pitch — MindPal
**MindPal** is a cognitive support platform designed to enhance:
- Focus  
- Mental clarity  
- Productivity  

The platform leverages **mood-based scheduling** and **intelligent analytics** to help users better understand and optimize their mental performance.

### Key Product Aspects
- Responsive and accessible web application  
- Real-time focus and mood tracking  
- Insight-driven analytics for productivity improvement  
- A solution built at the intersection of technology, empathy, and purpose
