# IEEE Kolkata Section Student Congress 2025  
### Best Student Chapter Award — IEEE IEM CS

## Event Overview
- **Event:** IEEE Kolkata Section Student Congress 2025  
- **Date:** August 29, 2025  
- **Representation:** IEEE IEM Computer Society Chapter  

On August 29, 2025, I had the privilege of representing **IEEE IEM CS** at the **IEEE Kolkata Section Student Congress 2025**. The event brought together passionate student leaders, innovators, and contributors from across the section, making it a deeply inspiring and enriching experience.

---

## Achievement
**IEEE IEM Computer Society** was honored with the **Best Student Chapter Award** at the Congress.

This recognition reflects the consistent efforts, initiatives, and collective dedication of the entire chapter toward technical growth, leadership, and community building.
