# Gen AI by Hand Workshop  
### Exploring the Real-World Impact of Generative AI

## Event Overview
- **Event:** Gen AI by Hand Workshop  
- **Organized by:** Hand Workshop × IEEE WIE Kolkata  
- **Focus:** Practical applications and future impact of Generative AI  

I had the opportunity to participate in the **“Gen AI by Hand” workshop**, an insightful event centered around understanding how **Generative AI** is shaping real-world workflows, productivity, and innovation.

---

## Key Highlights
- Expert-led sessions on workplace efficiency, model training, and the future of Generative AI  
- Talks by industry and academic leaders:
  - Dr. Anish Roychowdhury  
  - Prof. Tom Yeh  
  - Nilabhra Roy Chowdhury  
  - Aditya Sengupta  
- Official **India launch of the AI by Hand initiative**  
- A hands-on **Workbook Hackathon**, providing practical exposure to real AI tools and workflows  

---

## Learning Outcomes
Through the sessions and activities, I gained:
- Clear understanding of Generative AI workflows and implementation pipelines  
- Insights into how GenAI can be applied to real-world problems  
- Practical exposure through guided, hands-on exercises  
- Opportunities to interact with and learn from experienced mentors and peers  

---

## Takeaways
- Stronger conceptual clarity on Generative AI systems  
- Motivation to further explore AI-driven innovation  
- Valuable connections with passionate AI learners and professionals  
- Reinforced belief in learning through hands-on experimentation

## Looking Ahead
This workshop served as a strong foundation for deeper exploration into **AI innovation**, applied machine learning, and real-world GenAI systems. I look forward to building on these learnings in future projects and collaborations.
