# HexaFalls – Offline Software Hackathon  
### First Offline Hackathon Experience at JIS University

## Event Overview
- **Event:** HexaFalls  
- **Venue:** JIS University  
- **Dates:** June 28–29  
- **Duration:** 32 hours  
- **Type:** Offline Software Hackathon  

HexaFalls marked our **first offline software hackathon**, and it turned out to be a highly enriching and memorable experience. Over 32 continuous hours, the event was filled with learning, coding, collaboration, and growth.

---

##  Hackathon Journey
Although we did not secure a winning position, our team successfully delivered a **complete and functional solution** that aligned with every aspect of the given problem statement.

The journey involved:
- Structured brainstorming and ideation  
- End-to-end product development  
- Presenting our solution to mentors and judges  
- Receiving detailed feedback and encouragement  

The experience reinforced that learning and execution matter just as much as results.

---

##  What Made This Experience Special
- First-ever **offline hackathon exposure**  
- Insightful and constructive **mentor reviews**  
- Strong emphasis on **practical problem-solving**  
- Team bonding through continuous collaboration  
- A high-energy environment that encouraged growth and resilience
