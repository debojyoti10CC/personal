#  IEM Smart Makeathon  
### From Hacker to Organizer — A Leadership Milestone

##  Event Overview
- **Event:** IEM Smart Makeathon  
- **Festival:** Smart Maker’s Festival  
- **Dates:** September 6–7  
- **Venue:** IEM Saltlake  
- **Role:** Organizer & Core Team Member  

Until this point, hackathons had always meant pitching ideas, coding overnight, and presenting solutions.  
On **6th & 7th September**, that changed.

I stepped into an entirely new role — **organizing a hackathon** — and the experience taught me more than any participation ever could.

---

##  Scale & Impact
- **2000+ applications** received via Devfolio  
- **87 finalists** selected  
- **23 teams** competed in the final round  
- **24-hour offline finale**  

Managing an event at this scale was both **exciting and overwhelming**, pushing me far beyond my comfort zone.

---

##  Responsibilities & Execution
As part of the organizing team, I was involved across nearly every vertical:

- 🎨 Graphics & visual assets  
- 🤝 Community partnerships & collaborations  
- 📢 PR, outreach, and social media  
- 🧾 Database & participant handling  
- 📦 On-ground logistics & coordination  

From sleepless nights to last-minute problem-solving, every challenge demanded adaptability, ownership, and teamwork.

---

##  The Finale Experience
Despite the hardships, the finale was unforgettable.

Watching passionate innovators build, collaborate, and push boundaries reminded me **why hackathons matter** — not just as competitions, but as platforms for learning, creativity, and community-driven innovation.

The energy in the room made every effort worthwhile.
